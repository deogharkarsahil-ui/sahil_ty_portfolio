01 / Project overview

Less queue.
Easier access.

A concession form should not come down to reaching the counter at exactly the right time.

Students can face long queues at the transport office or miss the limited window when application forms are available. Created and developed by Sahil Dinesh Deogharkar, CollegeRide brings the application process online, so students can register, apply for a transport pass and track its progress in one place.

Its goal is a simpler, more accessible and transparent way to apply—with a paper-light path from application to printable pass.

02 / What it does

A pass, from first step
to print-ready.

One considered journey: student details, route choice, an application for review, and a printable result when approved.

SEVEN PROJECT FEATURES
01

Student registration

Register once with personal details, a passport-size photo and a security question.

02

Route & duration

Choose a bus route and the duration of the concession pass you need.

03

Automatic fee

The fee is calculated using the selected route's monthly fee.

04

Status tracking

Students can check whether their application has been approved or rejected.

05

Administrator review

The college administrator reviews applications and updates their status through phpMyAdmin.

06

Concession letter PDF

Approval generates a letter with student, route and pass details, a printed seal, college-stamp area and signature lines.

07

Printable pass card

The PDF also includes a compact card with the student's photo, route, validity dates and QR code.

03 / The application journey

Four steps to a
printable pass.

Students apply online; the college administrator reviews the application. A unique pass number and PDF are generated for approved applications.

01Start here

Register & sign in

The student creates an account with their details and logs in.

02Make a choice

Select route & duration

Choose a bus route and pass duration; the fee is calculated from its monthly fee.

03College review

Submit for review

The application is reviewed by the college administrator, who updates its status.

04If approved

Receive the approved pass

A unique pass number and printable PDF are generated for approved applications.
